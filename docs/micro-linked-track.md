# Micro linked tracks

English | [日本語](micro-linked-track_ja.md)

The micro linked-track system was developed by progressively reducing a larger prototype until it reached a 7 mm track width. It is a functional track system printed with a standard 0.4 mm-nozzle FDM printer and driven by 6 mm-diameter planetary geared motors.

A guide groove along the centre of each track works with openings shaped for the sprocket teeth. Together, these features keep the track aligned, transmit drive torque, and reduce derailment or slip.

This document is a design note, not a complete manufacturing procedure. It does not prescribe material, print settings, tolerances, or an assembly sequence; those depend on the equipment and parts used.

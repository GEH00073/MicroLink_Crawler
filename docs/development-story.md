# Development story

English | [日本語](development-story_ja.md)

The public project description records the following development progression:

1. An initial larger linked-track prototype.
2. Progressive miniaturisation to a 7 mm-wide linked track.
3. Drive by 6 mm-diameter planetary geared motors.
4. Wireless crawler control with Stamp Pico and Atom JoyStick.
5. Detachable AtomS3R-CAM FPV capability.
6. Core2 display of video plus orientation information.
7. Red-object detection and centre-coordinate display.
8. A complete system that fits in a book-sized carrying case.
9. Entry in the M5Stack Global Innovation Contest 2026 and Grand Prize recognition.

The public sources and repository do not establish a detailed chronology, design motivation, failed experiments, or fabrication conditions beyond these points. Those subjects are intentionally not reconstructed here.

Sources: [M5Stack contest results](https://m5stack.com/global-innovation-contest-2026/results) and the [Hackster.io project page](https://www.hackster.io/user2729037/microlink-crawler-379ff1).
